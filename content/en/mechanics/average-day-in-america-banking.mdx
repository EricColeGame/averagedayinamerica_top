## How Banking Works: Cash vs. Bank Balance

In the world of *Average Day In America*, money exists in two primary states: pocket cash and bank balance. Understanding the distinction dictates your entire survival loop. Carrying loose cash enables instant player-to-player transactions, underground vendor purchases, and illicit trades. However, pocket cash drops upon defeat, leaving you empty-handed after an ambush.

Conversely, money deposited into your bank account is tied directly to your character's persistent profile. Community reports show that while banked funds cannot be taken by typical street muggers, accessing them requires interacting with designated infrastructure across the map.

| Currency Type | Drop on Death? | Primary Use Cases | Accessibility | Security Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

Player experience demonstrates that the golden rule of wealth retention is simple: never carry more cash than you are willing to lose in a sudden firefight. Visiting safe financial hubs after every major score is the bedrock of consistent progress.

### Phase 1: Pre-Deposit Preparation
1. **Clear Your Perimeter:** Circle the banking block inside a vehicle before dismounting. Note any idling vehicles or loitering players.

3. **Queue Your Input:** Know your exact deposit amount before opening the menu to minimize menu dwelling time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can other players steal money directly from my bank account?
No. Other players cannot access your personal persistent balance. While criminals can rob the central vaults of El Banco for server-generated bounty caches, individual accounts cannot be hacked or drained by rivals. Your bank balance remains safe regardless of server events.

### What happens if I die while using an ATM?
If you are eliminated while the transaction menu is active, any cash that has not yet been confirmed as deposited will remain in your pocket inventory and drop on the ground. However, funds that completed the deposit process prior to your defeat remain secured in your account.

### Why does El Banco reject my cash deposit?
El Banco can reject deposits for three distinct reasons: the branch is undergoing an active robbery lockdown, the server-side daily deposit limit has been reached, or you are carrying "marked" illicit currency from an unlaundered heist. Illicit currency must be cleaned through laundering networks before conventional banks accept it.

### How do ATM fees compare to using the main branch in Average Day In America banking?
The main El Banco branch features zero transaction fees for standard deposits and withdrawals, making it the most cost-effective location for large transactions. In contrast, remote and suburban ATMs assess convenience fees ranging from 2.5% to 5.0% per transaction, balancing safety and convenience against financial efficiency.
