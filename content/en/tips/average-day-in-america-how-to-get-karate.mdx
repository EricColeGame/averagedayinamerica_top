### Step 1: Locate the Strip Mall Dojo
Head toward the Maplewood Strip Mall located just east of the city transit station. Behind the dry cleaner and next to the surplus outlet, you will spot an understated storefront marked with red kanji banners: the *Cobra-Sun Dojo*.

### Step 2: Speak with Sensei Vance
Interact with the head trainer, Sensei Vance, between 08:00 and 18:00 in-game time. If you approach outside these hours, the security shutter is pulled down. Select the dialogue option: *"I want to learn self-defense."*

Keep your guard raised and dash laterally whenever he winds up his signature spinning back-kick.

## Top Combos and Combat Strategy

Karate relies on quick inputs and rhythmic spacing. Unlike swinging a sledgehammer where wide hitboxes protect you from misses, karate demands accurate directional tracking.

### Recommended Combo Chains

*   **The Guard Shatter (Light - Light - Heavy):** Starts with two rapid palm strikes to confirm contact, followed by an overhand Axe Kick that instantly crushes blocking opponents.
*   **The Crowd Clearer (Sprint + Heavy into Light Sweep):** Execute a sliding low sweep to stagger three enemies simultaneously, then follow up with a quick heel stomp.
*   **The Perfect Counter (Block on Impact + Heavy):** Timing your block a split-second before an opponent strikes parries their arm, leaving them open to a devastating gut punch that inflicts an internal bleed debuff.

Maintaining spatial awareness is vital. While karate provides unmatched single-target DPS, getting surrounded by groups wielding bats or pipes can overwhelm your recovery frames. Always use side sweeps to herd groups into narrow corridors or against chain-link fences.
