## Core Mechanics: Dropping and Transferring Cash

Handling cash in an open-world survival environment is deliberately risky. Unlike traditional RPGs that feature secure, instanced player-to-player trade menus, *Average Day In America* prioritizes real-time, physicalized items. When you drop money, you instantiate a tangible cash prop on the ground that anyone nearby can snatch up.

To manage your physical wallet, follow the standard input sequences detailed below:

| Platform / Input | Action Trigger | Drop Execution | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PC (Keyboard/Mouse)** | Open Inventory (`Tab` or `B`) | Drag Cash Icon outside window / Click `Drop` | Hold `Shift` while dragging to specify an exact sum. |
| **PC Chat Command** | Press `/` to open chat | Type `/drop [amount]` (e.g., `/drop 500`) | Works only when standing outside of safe zone menus. |
| **Console (Controller)** | Press `D-Pad Down` / View Inventory | Highlight Wallet -> Press `X` / `Square` | Prompts a slider modal to choose custom quantity. |
| **Quick Toss Keybind** | Hotbar assigned slot | Press `Backspace` while holding cash tool | Drops the currently equipped denomination immediately. |

Community reports indicate that drop commands can experience minor input lag if server ping spikes above 120ms. Always confirm the money bundle hits the ground before closing your inventory menu.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### In Average Day In America how to drop money if the chat command doesn't work?
If the `/drop [amount]` chat command fails, open your character inventory using `Tab` (or `B`), click on your cash balance, and manually drag it outside your inventory window or select the physical "Drop" button. Make sure your combat tag has expired before attempting this.

### Can other players steal money while I am dropping it?
Yes. Dropped money creates a physical item on the floor that any nearby survivor can claim. Always make drops in enclosed, secure areas or coordinate directly with trusted teammates to prevent drive-by thefts.

### Is there a maximum limit on how much money you can drop at once?
Most servers enforce single-transaction caps to deter exploits and duping. Check your current server rules, but typical limits range between $5,000 and $10,000 per dropped container. Larger transfers require multiple consecutive drops or formal bank transfers.

### Do I lose dropped money if the server restarts?
Yes. Unclaimed physical cash props on the ground are completely wiped whenever a server resets, crashes, or unloads a zone chunk. Never leave money sitting on the floor unattended.
