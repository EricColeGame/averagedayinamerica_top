## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I fully remap the Average Day In America console controls on PlayStation and Xbox?
Yes. The in-game settings menu offers several preset controller layouts (such as Tactical, Southpaw, and Bumper Jumper), as well as a full custom binding menu where you can assign almost every foot and driving action to your preferred buttons.

### Does the game support keyboard and mouse input on consoles?
While native console support is primarily built around gamepads, player experience indicates that basic keyboard and mouse functionality works for menu navigation, but aiming may lack native mouse smoothing unless configured through an authorized adapter.

### Why do the Average Day In America console controls feel unresponsive while driving?
Driving unresponsiveness is typically caused by high steering deadzones or vehicle handling degradation. Lower your Left Stick inner deadzone to 5–7% in the controller menu and ensure your vehicle tires aren't damaged, as tire condition directly impacts analog turn radius.
