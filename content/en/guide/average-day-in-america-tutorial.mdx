## Core Mechanics and Status Meters Explained

Before sprinting into town, you must understand your character’s vitals. The simulation treats every minor inconvenience as a potential crisis. Neglecting any single meter triggers severe debuffs that impair combat agility and movement speed.

### Understanding the Survival HUD

Your display tracks four critical vitals: Health, Stamina, Hunger, and Stress. Stress acts as a multiplier: high stress drains stamina rapidly and reduces your accuracy during chaotic encounters.

| Status Meter | Depletion Cause | Restoration Method | Failure Consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Health** | Physical trauma, traffic accidents, starvation | Medkits, bandages, quality sleep | Character death / hospital respawn fee |
| **Stamina** | Sprinting, heavy melee strikes, jumping | Rest, energy drinks, passive sitting | Inability to dodge or flee combat |
| **Hunger** | Passive passage of time, strenuous physical labor | Diner meals, grocery snacks, vending items | Gradual HP decay and stamina cap reduction |
| **Stress** | Sirens, active gunfire, unpaid bills, low sleep | Arcade minigames, coffee, watching TV | Screen blur, weapon sway, increased panic |

Community reports highlight that managing your Stress bar is often more critical than stocking up on heavy ammunition. When panic sets in, even basic interactions take twice as long to execute.

## Navigating the Law and City Heat Levels

Causing property damage, carrying open weapons, or driving on sidewalks increases your Heat Level. Law enforcement response scales rapidly, moving from single patrol cars to full tactical units.

```
Heat 1 (Verbal Warning / Fine) ➔ Heat 2 (Baton Pursuit) ➔ Heat 3 (Roadblocks) ➔ Heat 4 (Tactical Helicopters)
```

### De-escalation Techniques

- **Holster Instantly:** Press your weapon swap key twice to conceal arms before law enforcement gains line of sight.
- **Break Line of Sight:** Duck through laundromats, alleys, or subway entrances.
- **Pay Tickets Promptly:** Fines left unpaid for more than 48 in-game hours trigger an automated bounty on your character.

| Heat Level | Law Enforcement Unit | Primary Tactic | Best Evasion Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Mall Security / Foot Patrol | Verbal orders, citations | Comply, holster weapon, pay immediate fine |
| **Level 2** | Local Police Cruiser | Taser, aggressive pursuit | Vault low fences, cut through tight alleyways |
| **Level 3** | SWAT Van Patrols | High-caliber engagement | Enter sewer networks or hide in safehouse attics |
| **Level 4** | State Helicopters & Armored Cars | Searchlights, perimeter blockades | Change outfits at clothing stores; wait out cooldown |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where can I find the best defensive weapons early in the game?
Look through hardware store backrooms, construction site lockers, and pawn shops. Pawn shops carry sturdy blunt weapons and occasionally used sidearms at deep discounts.

### How does this Average Day In America tutorial recommend lowering stress fast?
The fastest non-medicinal way to reduce stress is visiting the 24-hour arcade to play minigames, followed by taking a hot shower in your apartment. Consuming luxury foods like diner pie also provides an instant temporary stress buffer.

### What happens if my character dies during an active job shift?
You will respawn at the nearest county hospital with a medical bill deducted from your account. Your employer will terminate your shift, meaning you must wait until the following morning to reapply or collect remaining wages.

### Can I run this game completely non-lethally?
Yes. By relying on tasers, pepper spray, evasion tactics, and high-stamina builds, players can complete entire storylines and side jobs without killing a single NPC.
