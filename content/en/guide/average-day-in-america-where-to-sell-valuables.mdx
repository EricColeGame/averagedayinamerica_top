## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the best Average Day In America where to sell valuables spot located?
The single verified location to liquidate your trade loot is Easy Pawn. While places like Bloxmart and Newbury Police Station are major landmarks across town, neither vendor accepts gray-market electronics or contraband files.

### What items count as valuables in Average Day In America?
Items specifically classified under the **Pawnable** category can be sold. Based on current game data, this primarily includes recovered USB drives and government or corporate Files. Functional tools, consumables, and explosives cannot be pawned.

### Can other players kill me inside Easy Pawn?
Yes. Easy Pawn is not a protected safe zone. Hostile players can follow you inside, shoot through storefront glass, or camp inside the shop to steal your backpack before you complete your sale.

### Can I sell weapons or knives at the pawn shop?
No. Kitchen knives, firearms, and explosive ordinance like Dynamite or F1 Grenades cannot be pawned at Easy Pawn. Weapons must be used in combat, stored in your private safehouse/vehicle, or discarded to free up backpack space.
