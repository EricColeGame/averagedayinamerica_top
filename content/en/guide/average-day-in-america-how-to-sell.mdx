## Core Selling Mechanics: How the In-Game Economy Works

Trading in the game relies on decentralized commerce rather than a universal auction house. Different non-player characters (NPCs) and kiosks scattered across town prioritize distinct item categories. Bringing industrial scrap to a general grocer will net you pennies, while selling it to a mechanic or scrap yard operator yields maximum value.

To initiate a transaction:
1. Approach an authorized merchant or interactive pawn terminal.
2. Interact with the seller prompt (default keybind: `E` on keyboard / `X` or `Square` on controller).
3. Toggle from the "Buy" interface to the "Sell" tab at the top of the menu screen.
4. Select individual items or use the bulk-sell feature for common salvage.
5. Confirm the transaction to deposit cash directly into your wallet.

Understanding durability loss is also vital. Damaged equipment, broken firearms, and expired consumables carry steep pricing penalties. Whenever possible, repair high-tier machinery before liquidation to maximize return on investment.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Liquidating Inventory

Player experience logs highlight several recurring blunders that can drain your prospective profits:

- **Dumping everything at convenience stores:** While convenient, residential mom-and-pop stores offer the worst profit margins in the game. Only sell perishables there.
- **Selling un-appraised lockboxes:** Opening safes and locked containers before selling their contents almost always yields rare gems, notes, or contraband worth far more than the sealed container itself.
- **Neglecting Barter perks:** Investing skill points into trade and negotiation trees pays dividends early. Even a 5% price boost accumulates into thousands of bonus dollars across multiple play sessions.
- **Overlooking carry weight limits:** Becoming encumbered while hauling heavy scrap to an industrial scrapper slows movement speed, exposing you to street hazards and rival players.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### In Average Day In America how to sell stolen or contraband items without getting caught?
To liquidate contraband, illegal narcotics, or stolen high-value loot, avoid public pawn shops and government checkposts. Instead, seek out Black Market Fences located in back alleyways or secluded docks after dark. Fences do not report stolen goods, though they may demand a slight laundering fee depending on your street reputation.

### Can I buy back an item if I accidentally sell it?
Yes, most standard vendors feature a temporary "Buyback" tab at the bottom of their transaction window. However, this buffer resets once you leave the area or the daily shop stock refreshes. Furthermore, buying back an item costs the full retail price rather than the discounted rate you received when selling it.

### Do vendor payouts reset or run out of cash?
Yes. Major vendors operate with a finite cash pool that refreshes every 24 in-game hours. If you exhaust a vendor's available cash reserves, you must either purchase items from them to replenish their funds or wait until the next economic cycle resets their cash pool.
