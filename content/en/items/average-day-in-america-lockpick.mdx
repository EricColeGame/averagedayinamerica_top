## How to Use the Lockpick: Mechanics and Mini-Games

Once you equip an **Average Day In America lockpick**, interacting with a locked doorway or container triggers an entry sequence. Successfully completing the interaction unlocks the target without creating the loud signature caused by explosives like Dynamite or an F1 Grenade.

| Breaching Method | Noise Level | Speed | Reusability | Target Compatibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lockpick** | Silent / Near-Silent | 4–8 Seconds | Chance to break | Padlocks, Interior Doors, Safes |
| **Armoury Key** | Instant | Instant | Permanent (Specific) | Dedicated Armory Doors |
| **Dynamite** | Deafening | 3-Second Fuse | Single-use | Reinforced Walls, Metal Gates |
| **F1 Grenade** | Deafening | 4-Second Fuse | Single-use | Soft Wooden Doors, Crowd Control |

### Step-by-Step Bypassing Guide

1. **Equip from Hotbar**: Slot your pick into your primary action bar and approach the yellow or red interaction prompt on the target door.
2. **Engage the Lock**: Hold your primary interaction key (`E` by default on PC). 
3. **Monitor the Tension Meter**: Keep your input steady within the green target zone. Rushing the process increases the chance of snapping the tool.
4. **Slip Inside Quickly**: Once the tumbler clicks, sprint inside and shut the door behind you to conceal your presence from patrolling guards or rival players.

## Lockpick vs. Explosives: Which Breaching Tool Wins?

When planning a raid on an enemy stash or an NPC-guarded facility, deciding between stealth and brute force determines whether you escape clean or get swarmed by the entire server.

```
Breaching Approach Tree:
├── Stealth Approach (Lockpick)
│   ├── No server-wide sound cues
│   ├── Retains structural cover for defensive holds
│   └── Slower entry speed (Vulnerable during pick mini-game)
│
```

While tossing an F1 Grenade or laying a stick of Dynamite takes doors down instantly, it broadcasts your exact coordinates across the block. Utilizing an **Average Day In America lockpick** ensures you slip in, clear the cash registers and safe deposits, and slip away before anyone realizes the perimeter was breached.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the Average Day In America lockpick break during use?
Yes. Based on player experience, failing the tension mini-game or using a tool on high-security locks carries a distinct failure rate that can snap the pick. Always carry a backup or have an exit route planned if the tool breaks mid-heist.

### Does using a lockpick alert NPC guards automatically?
No. Unlike explosives such as Dynamite, lockpicking does not trigger global sound alerts. However, if a guard NPC or rival player walks directly into your line of sight while you are picking the lock, they will become hostile immediately.

### Where is the best place to sell items obtained from lockpicked rooms?
Items labeled under the pawnable category, such as USB drives and confidential files, should be taken directly to the Easy Pawn shop counter. Selling these goods provides the highest raw cash return in Newbury.

### Can an Average Day In America lockpick open police armory doors?
Standard picks work on basic interior doors, offices, and safes. However, high-security sectors like the main weapons depot inside the Newbury Police Station typically require a specialized Armoury Key or high-yield explosives to breach.
