## Troubleshooting Keycard Failures and Glitches

In some game sessions or custom community servers, players encounter technical problems when interacting with electronic keypads. Understanding how game mechanics interact with the server helps prevent wasted attempts.

### Common Issues and Fixes

* **"Access Denied" Error:** Double-check the tier level. Attempting to swipe a Blue **Average Day In America keycard** on a Level 3 Red Vault terminal will result in an immediate error and trigger an alert buzzer.
* **Card Depletion:** Ensure your card has remaining uses. Community reports confirm that certain rare cards feature durability meters that degrade after 1 to 3 successful swipes.
* **Terminal Power Outages:** Some specialized bunkers require players to restart an external diesel generator before the electronic card reader will accept input.
* **Desync Issues:** If the terminal does not respond to your interaction key, switch weapons or drop and pick up the keycard to resynchronize your inventory state with the server host.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the Average Day In America keycard disappear after one use?
It depends on the tier and server configuration. Common Green keycards frequently break or get consumed upon opening a door, while rare Black or Red keycards generally grant multiple uses or persist throughout an entire gameplay session unless dropped upon death.

### Can I craft an Average Day In America keycard at a workbench?
In standard versions of the game, security keycards cannot be crafted from raw materials; they must be scavenged from the map or looted from defeated bosses and high-ranking security guards. Certain heavily modded survival servers may introduce custom electronics schematics, but base settings require field discovery.

### What should I do if an alarm sounds when I swipe my card?
Expect immediate hostility. High-security doors—particularly bank vaults and military chambers—feature automatic defensive countermeasures that alert nearby AI patrols and broadcast audible audio cues to nearby players. Take defensive cover immediately while the vault cycles open.
